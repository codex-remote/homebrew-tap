# Codex Remote 0.2.0-beta.6

Codex Remote `0.2.0-beta.6` is an unsigned, unnotarized Apple Silicon Beta.
It replaces the oversized terminal pairing QR introduced in Beta 5 with a
smaller renderer designed around how phone cameras see terminal cells.

## Fixed

- The default terminal QR now fits in roughly 47 x 24 terminal cells.
- Solid modules use pure black or pure white ANSI background cells instead of
  full-block glyphs that can leave font-rendered seams.
- Mixed vertical modules use explicit foreground/background half blocks to
  preserve approximately square QR modules.
- An extra white module surrounds the encoder's existing quiet zone to isolate
  the QR from nearby terminal text.
- `large`, `compact`, and `small` renderers remain explicit alternatives.

The final ANSI output was reconstructed as a pure black/white bitmap and
successfully detected and decoded with OpenCV before publication.

## Compatibility

- macOS Apple Silicon only.
- Codex CLI `0.148.0` or later; tested with `codex-cli 0.154.0-alpha.6.2`.
- iPhone Safari on the same trusted local network.

SHA256 is published as a separate release asset alongside the archive and
manifest.
